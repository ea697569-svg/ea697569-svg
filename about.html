About OURS / GIF

Last updated: 28 October 2026

OURS / GIF is a small web project built to make it easier to explore reaction visuals, search a starter collection, and create short GIFs from videos in a browser.

What you can do here

  • Search items by ID or topic.
  • Browse the collection using reaction and category menus.
  • Open an item to view its preview and available copy or download actions.
  • Try simple GIF games and create a GIF from a video you are authorised to use.

About the collection

The library is intentionally a work in progress. Item descriptions are provided to make browsing clearer, but they do not establish ownership or permission to reuse any image. We aim to add original work and material that is properly licensed or used with permission.

Our approach

We want the site to be easy to navigate, useful, accessible on mobile devices, and respectful of creators' rights. If you spot a broken item or have a rights-related concern, please use the contact details on the Contact page.
