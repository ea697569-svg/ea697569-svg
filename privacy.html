Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy describes the current OURS / GIF website in plain language. Update it whenever the site's features, hosting, analytics or advertising arrangements change. This page is information, not a guarantee of compliance with every law in every location.

Information you provide

The static GIF library does not require an account. If you contact the site owner by email, the information in your message and email address will be received by the email provider and the site owner so the request can be answered.

Browser storage

Some interactive features may store local preferences or game counts in your browser using local storage. This information is stored on your device and is not, by itself, a server-side account.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted as a static website. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser details and request logs for security, reliability and operation. Consult the hosting provider's current privacy documentation for details.

Advertising and cookies

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled in the future, that service and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure or personalise ads, subject to the settings, consent requirements and policies that apply. Do not assume advertising is active merely because this section describes it. Before enabling ads, configure the required privacy disclosures and consent tools for the audiences and regions you serve.

Third-party services

The page currently loads Bootstrap and GIF-creation code from a content delivery network. Those services may receive technical request information when a browser downloads their files. Review the current provider policies and consider hosting dependencies locally if you prefer to reduce third-party requests.

Your choices

You can clear your browser's local storage and cookies through your browser settings. If you contact the site owner, you may request that your message be deleted where applicable, subject to legitimate record-keeping needs.

Children

The site is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not submit information about yourself or someone else that is not needed to contact the site owner.

Changes

This policy should be reviewed and updated when the site changes. The date at the top should reflect the date of the most recent substantive update.
